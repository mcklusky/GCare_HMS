# 15 — Data Retention, Export and Exit Plan

## Governance
Retention and deletion periods are jurisdiction- and record-type-specific decisions approved by accountable legal/privacy and clinical owners. This template does not set statutory periods. Maintain a schedule covering clinical records, billing, audit, identity, logs, backups, attachments, DICOM studies, exports and temporary migration files.

## Retention controls
For each class record purpose, owner, legal basis, retention trigger/period, archive method, access scope, legal hold, deletion method, backup expiry and audit evidence. Prevent routine deletion where clinical/financial integrity or legal hold applies. Ensure deletion from replicas/backups follows documented lifecycle and vendor capabilities.

## Export
Provide authorized, logged exports in documented, non-proprietary formats with data dictionary, identifiers, timestamps/timezones, provenance, relationships, configuration and audit extracts as agreed. Include DICOM objects/metadata where contractually applicable. Validate completeness, integrity/checksums, readability and transfer security with customer recipient.

## Customer exit
Define notice, export scope, format, cost/assistance, delivery method, transition period, access during transition, final reconciliation, retention after termination, deletion certification and handling of backups/legal holds. Preserve accountable ownership and prevent uncontrolled vendor copies. Test export/restore periodically before exit is needed.

## Approval evidence
Store approved retention schedule, export schema/version, access approvals, transfer logs, deletion/retention evidence, legal hold records and customer acceptance.
