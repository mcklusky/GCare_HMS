# 14 — User Training and Support Plan

## Roles and curriculum
Tailor training for registration, triage/nursing, clinicians, radiology technologists, radiologists, cashiers/finance, administrators, auditors and support. Cover identity verification, role boundaries, patient search, workflow tasks, errors, downtime, privacy, audit and escalation. Avoid live patient data in training; use approved synthetic scenarios.

## Readiness
Training materials match the deployed version and approved workflows. Use role-based practice and competency checks for high-risk tasks: patient duplicate resolution, signing/amendment, critical-result acknowledgement, refunds/reversals, break-glass and downtime reconciliation. Record attendance, completion, role and date; provide refresher process.

## Support model
Document support hours, channels, severity definitions, initial response targets, escalation contacts, vendor dependencies and customer/vendor responsibilities. Support personnel use named accounts and least privilege; time-bound elevated access requires approval and audit. Do not request patient details in unsecured support channels.

## Knowledge and continuous improvement
Maintain quick guides, known issues, release notes, troubleshooting and FAQs with version/date/owner. Review support trends and incidents with department owners; feed approved changes into backlog and retraining. Define onboarding for new staff and offboarding/access revocation.
